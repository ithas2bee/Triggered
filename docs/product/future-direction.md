# Future Product Direction

Future stories may explore context including quantity, meal size, time of day,
time before sleep, other foods, ingredients, and repeated contextual patterns.
Ingredient-level correlation and AI-assisted personal insights are possible
future directions.

For example, future analysis could compare reactions after a food is consumed
in a large meal late at night. This is a product direction, not an MVP
requirement. None of these capabilities should be implemented without an
explicit future user story and appropriate architectural decisions.
